**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Program Title:** 30-Day Habit & Vitality Transformation  

---

## 1. 30-Day Pedagogical Periodization

The FitLife 30-day program is structured into 4 cohesive weekly arcs plus a 2-day finale:

- **Week 1: Build the Habit (Days 1–7)**  
  Focus on neuromuscular activation, baseline posture, joint lubrication, and foundational breathing.
- **Week 2: Build Consistency (Days 8–14)**  
  Introduction of gentle compound combinations, balance drills, and aerobic conditioning.
- **Week 3: Improve Strength & Endurance (Days 15–21)**  
  Progressive overload through extended interval duration, reduced rest, and greater core stability.
- **Week 4: Maintain Consistency & Peak Mobility (Days 22–28)**  
  Integrating functional movements that replicate daily life: lifting, rotating, reaching, and balancing.
- **Days 29–30: Reflection, Celebration & Sustainable Habit Review (Days 29–30)**  
  Consolidating gains, personal reflection, setting lifelong activity intentions.

## 3. Rest Day Protocol

Rest days (Days 7, 14, 21, 28) are vital components of the program:
- They allow micro-tears in muscle tissue to synthesize protein and rebuild stronger.
- They prevent chronic nervous system fatigue.
- In FitLife, rest days are not marked as "do nothing" days — they feature an uplifting recovery card offering hydration advice, gentle 3-minute mobility stretching, and mindfulness affirmations.
